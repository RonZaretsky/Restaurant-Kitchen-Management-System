- (event) Competitive research digest: KDS platforms (Toast, Square, TouchBistro, Lightspeed, Oracle MICROS) treat kitchen display as real-time pub/sub of order events to station queues, color/timer-coded tickets, inventory deduction usually a secondary integration not a core transactional guarantee. No mainstream KDS bundles AI recipe-suggestion-from-stock; that pattern exists separately in consumer food-waste apps (SuperCook, Mealime) and enterprise waste-analytics (Winnow, Leanpath) but not combined with B2B kitchen inventory -- flagged as a genuine differentiator for this project's Smart Chef feature. Common pitfalls: staff adoption friction, shallow system integration forcing manual reconciliation, offline/connectivity dependency.
